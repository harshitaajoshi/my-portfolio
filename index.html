About Me

Where I study and what I care about.

I’m a B.S. candidate in Computer Science and Mathematics at Texas Tech University’s Honors College, graduating in Spring 2028. I hold a Presidential Merit Scholarship and carry a 3.9 GPA, with coursework in data structures and algorithms, object-oriented programming, operating systems, and software engineering.

Most of what I enjoy sits at the point where a program meets the machine: query engines, memory layout, graph search, and real-time rendering. I learn these by shipping into real codebases rather than reading about them, which is why most of my work lives in public repositories and merged pull requests.

Outside of coursework I want to keep building developer tools — especially tools that make a large, unfamiliar codebase legible to the person who just joined it.
